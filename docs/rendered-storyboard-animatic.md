# Rendered storyboard and animatic gate

G2 now closes after rights, IP bible, script, and claims approval. This lets the
reusable asset factory approve exact visual, voice, and music references before
the storyboard/animatic approval gate.

The Studio **Storyboard / Animatic** workbench records a typed plan bound to the
current creative versions, every shot and duration, immutable asset versions,
temporary voice/music, captions, transitions, provider/model identity, retry
policy, and cost ceiling. An explicitly authorized native render creates a
storyboard contact sheet and full timed MP4. Failed attempts remain durable and
can resume only within the recorded retry limit.

Named-human approval adopts the immutable render as the canonical animatic.
G3 requires that render, its budget receipt, current lineage, storyboard
approval, and independent producer/finance routing approval. Routed shot
requests and production preflight bind its video digest so expensive generation
cannot drift from reviewed timing.

The deterministic renderer is planning media, not commercial footage. It
performs a local process effect, no provider network effect, and grants no
publication authority.
