# Systematic long-form production

## Commercial intake before G0

Production begins with `qualified lead → versioned scoped quote → commercial approval + production approval → client acceptance → finite capacity reservation → draft project conversion`. The accepted quote binds the payable problem, measurable acceptance, duration, deliverables, revision policy, overages, milestones, restrictions, dates, margin forecast, founder-hours, and capacity plan.

Conversion is not G0 approval. It creates the draft `project → episode → scene → shot → asset` root and prepopulates the brief, pending rights intake, budget, schedule, milestones, restrictions, and economics. No creative or downstream authority is carried forward. G0 additionally requires a human approval of the exact brief whose intake digest still matches the accepted quote boundary.

Mooncast v2 owns the complete project → episode → scene → shot → asset → master
→ delivery lifecycle. Every transition writes immutable evidence.

1. **G0 Brief:** accept objective, audience, channels, 3–8 minute target, and
   acceptance baseline.
2. **G1 Rights + bible:** clear source/derivative rights and lock the world,
   character, and style bible.
3. **G2 Creative:** approve script, storyboard, and duration-accurate animatic.
4. **G3 Production authorization:** approve the exact versioned model-routing
   plan, per-asset provider/model/data class, fallbacks, retries, unit estimates,
   and total ceiling through separate named producer and finance decisions.
5. Generate deterministic or external shot attempts. Preserve every selected
   shot's provider, model, prompt, parameters, rights, labels, and SHA-256.
   Production Provider Jobs are created from the exact G3 plan, one configured
   video adapter per shot, with route retry/cost ceilings and aggregate budget.
   Successful ingestion atomically binds Job/config/plan/attempt/artifact/cost
   evidence into the canonical shot attempt; missing host transport remains a
   resumable setup state. The legacy generator is a local fixture only.
6. **G4 Shot QC:** pass only when every planned shot has immutable successful
   attempt lineage and exact passes for technical, continuity, safety, claims,
   and rights findings.
7. Assemble the master timeline with audio, subtitles, visible AI labels, and
   shot lineage. Run duration, stream, continuity, safety, and technical QC.
8. Build a versioned delivery package. **G5 Final + client acceptance:** require
   technical-QC, creative-director, rights/compliance, and client approvals
   bound to the exact master plus exact delivery-milestone acceptance.
9. Build the customer delivery: deterministic horizontal/vertical variants,
   bounded channel cuts, covers, subtitles, metadata, rights/provenance/AI-label
   manifest, checksums, and an immutable ZIP. A named human accepts the exact
   build digest; upstream master, package, or G5 changes invalidate it.
10. Create a revocable, expiring client review link for that exact master and
    build. Collect timecode annotations and the exact client decision. Unlock
    the ZIP only after acceptance; record billing milestones without executing
    payment. A repeat order starts a clean project with new brief/rights review.
11. **G6 Publication authorization:** record a separate authorization for every
   delivery destination, bound to the accepted ZIP build. Authorization never
   performs publication.
12. Observe external publication receipts if publication occurs. **G7
    Performance review:** bind performance, economics, founder-hours, and
    repeat-purchase results to the exact delivery/publication evidence; record
    an explicit unavailable or waived reason when unpublished.

Retries create attempts; they never overwrite an asset. Any upstream mutation
clears affected downstream gate receipts and appends an invalidation record;
accepted record history remains immutable.
