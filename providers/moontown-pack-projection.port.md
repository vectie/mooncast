# MoonTown pack projection provider

MoonCast publishes `moontown.pack-projection.v1` as a read-only view of
creative briefs, production projects, delivery gates, and control-tower
exceptions. Provider execution remains behind MoonCast's reviewed operations.

Bottom-up ideas enter through `creative.candidate.intake`, which prepares a
reviewable creative brief. It cannot create a project, generate an asset,
publish media, or accept a delivery milestone.
