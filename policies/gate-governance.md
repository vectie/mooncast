# G0–G7 gate governance

Gates must pass in order. Each receipt names an actor, project revision,
timestamp, and SHA-256 evidence digest. Rights, dual budget authority,
five-dimension per-shot QC, exact-master review, client milestone acceptance,
destination-specific publication authority, and performance/economics review
fail closed. Generation, assembly, packaging, analytics, and authorization are
evidence-producing operations and never approve themselves. An agent may
prepare evidence but may not impersonate a human acceptor. Any upstream change
invalidates downstream gate receipts without deleting immutable history.
